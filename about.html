My Pappy, where it all began

My Story

Why I Do This Work

I grew up watching my granddad take his health into his own hands, managing Parkinson's and type 2 diabetes through diet and lifestyle. Our whole family felt the ripple of what one educated, empowered patient could do.

In critical care, I kept seeing preventable conditions that could have been caught earlier. So I became a family nurse practitioner, completed HTMA Expert training, became a certified Brain Health Professional through Amen University, and completed Functional Diagnostic Nutrition's Pediatric Supplementation training.

We don't have to overcomplicate it. Often, it's just going back to the basics.

My Own Experience

Why I Start With Testing

I've had my own seasons of exhaustion, hormone, and sleep struggles. Specific testing is what made my own plan clear: balanced minerals, whole-food nutrition focused on what my body actually needed (no fads), and a few targeted supplements.

Today, postpartum with two kids, I have more energy than I did in my first pregnancy. Every body is different, which is why I start with testing.

My Approach

Why Intention

I created Intention Holistic Health for moms who've been told they're fine but don't feel fine, and for parents searching for answers for their kids.

This is functional medicine rooted in the basics, not endless detoxes, cleanses, or 20-supplement routines. I work alongside your primary care provider, OB-GYN, midwife, or pediatrician, not in place of them.
