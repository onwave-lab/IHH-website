## 1. What changed and why

The practice is adding **Kids Mineral Insight**, an HTMA-based wellness service for **Kentucky children ages 4–17**. The current website legal pages were written for an adults-only practice. Page 7 of the Privacy Policy said, "Our services are not directed toward individuals under age 18."

**How the practice will run the kids service (Alicia's decisions):**
- Kentucky residents only, and the child must be physically in Kentucky during each telehealth visit.
- A parent or guardian takes part in **every** visit, at every age.
- Parents and guardians receive the **full** results, written report, and plan for every child, at every age.
- The service works alongside the child's pediatrician. It is not primary care, well-child care, vaccinations, or physicals.
- All child health information is collected only through **Practice Better**, the HIPAA-compliant portal. It is never collected through website forms, email lists, or social media.
- Results reviews may be recorded **with written consent**. The recording is a private screen walkthrough of the written plan, shared only through Practice Better.

### 3A. Notice of Privacy Practices: addendum for minors
> **Minors and Personal Representatives.** When a client is under 18, the client's parent or legal guardian is generally treated as the client's personal representative under HIPAA. The parent or guardian may access, receive copies of, and request amendments to the child's health information, and may authorize its disclosure, except where Kentucky or federal law gives the minor the right to consent to certain care on their own or otherwise limits parental access. A parent or guardian who has legal authority to make health decisions for the child must provide documentation of that authority on request. In cases of divorce, separation, or custody orders, we may ask for a copy of the relevant order. If we believe a child may be at risk of harm, we will follow Kentucky law, which may include reporting to the appropriate authorities.

### 3B. Parent/Guardian Consent to Care (Kids Mineral Insight)
> I am the parent or legal guardian of the child named above and have the legal authority to consent to their care. If there is a custody order, I confirm it allows me to make health decisions for this child.
>
> I consent to: a hair tissue mineral analysis (HTMA) using a hair sample I collect at home; review of the health history, food diary, and other information I provide; telehealth visits; and individualized nutrition and wellness recommendations for my child.
>
> I understand that:
> - HTMA is a screening tool, not a diagnostic test, and does not diagnose any condition.
> - This service works alongside, and does not replace, my child's pediatrician or primary care provider. It does not include well-child visits, vaccinations, physicals, urgent, emergency, or after-hours care.
> - I (or another authorized parent or guardian) will take part in every visit, and my child will be physically located in Kentucky during each visit.
> - I will receive my child's full results, written report, and plan.
> - Results vary, and no specific outcome is guaranteed.
> - I should share these results with my child's pediatrician before making major changes, and call 911 for an emergency.

### 3C. Child/Teen Assent (age-banded)
**Ages 4–10 (read aloud by parent):**
> Alicia is going to look at a tiny bit of your hair to learn about the minerals in your body. She'll talk with you and your grown-up about foods and habits that may help you feel good. You can ask any questions you want.

**Ages 11–17 (teen signs):**
> We'll explain your results and plan in a way that makes sense to you, and you're welcome to ask questions and share your own goals. Your parent or guardian will be at every visit and will see your results and plan. If something you share makes us worried about your safety, we may need to tell a parent or another appropriate adult. I understand and agree to take part.

### 3D. Telehealth Consent: minors addendum
> For a client under 18, the child must be physically located in Kentucky at the time of each telehealth visit, and a parent or legal guardian must take part in the entire visit. If the child is not in Kentucky, or no parent or guardian is present, the visit will be rescheduled. Telehealth has limits, including no physical exam, and may not be appropriate for every concern.

### 3E. Recording Consent
> With your consent, Alicia will create a private screen recording that walks through your (or your child's) written plan. The recording shows the plan document, not you or your child. It is shared only with you through the secure Practice Better portal, will be available for **[__ days/months — counsel to set]**, and will then be deleted. It is for your personal use or for sharing with a co-parent or guardian involved in the child's care. Please do not post or share it publicly. You may decline or withdraw consent at any time without affecting care.

### 3F. Portal Access for Minors
> The parent or guardian manages the Practice Better account for a child under 18. Messages, forms, results, and recordings are sent to the parent or guardian. Teens do not have separate portal accounts at this time.

## 4. Questions for counsel

1. **Parental access to records.** Kentucky allows minors to consent on their own to certain care (for example, some reproductive, mental health, and substance use services). This service doesn't provide that care. Is "parents receive full records, except where Kentucky or federal law requires otherwise" enough? Or should we describe specific carve-outs?
2. **Custody situations.** Is one parent's consent enough for this kind of wellness service, or should we require both parents' consent when there's joint custody? What documentation should we request?
3. **Parent present at every visit.** Is "takes part in every visit" the right standard for all ages? Or should older teens (16–17) have any one-on-one time?
4. **Mandatory reporting.** Is the safety-exception wording correct for an APRN in Kentucky?
5. **Recording retention.** How long should recordings be kept? Should they be part of the medical record?
6. **COPPA.** We collect no data from children through the website. Is the "directed to adults" statement enough?
7. **Scope and malpractice.** Please confirm that pediatric HTMA wellness services fit within an FNP's scope of practice in Kentucky. Alicia will separately confirm malpractice coverage for pediatric clients.
8. **Governing law.** The Terms and Privacy Policy name **Texas** for the business entity and **Kentucky** for clinical services. Is this still correct?
9. **Waitlist form.** Kids Mineral Insight is currently a waitlist (Practice Better form). Are there consent or privacy notices needed on the waitlist form itself?
10. **Discovery Session credit.** "$100 is credited toward Mineral Insight or The Intention Method if you enroll within 30 days." Are any additional terms needed?
