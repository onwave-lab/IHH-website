**INTENTION HOLISTIC HEALTH PLLC: NOTICE OF PRIVACY PRACTICES**

**THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.**

Effective Date: [DATE]

**Our Duties**
We are required by law to keep your protected health information (PHI) private, to give you this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, to follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect, and to notify you if a breach affects your unsecured PHI.

**How We May Use and Share Your Health Information**

- **Treatment.** We may use your information to provide your care and may share it with other providers involved in your care, such as your primary care provider or your child's pediatrician.
- **Payment.** We may use your information to bill and collect payment for services. We do not bill insurance.
- **Health care operations.** We may use your information to run our practice, improve care, and contact you when needed.
- **Business associates.** We work with companies that help us operate, such as our client portal (Practice Better), the HTMA laboratory, and our supplement dispensary (Fullscript). They must sign agreements to protect your information.
- **Laboratory.** We share the information needed to process your HTMA sample with the laboratory.

**Other Uses and Disclosures Allowed or Required by Law**
We may share your information without your written authorization when required or permitted by law, including:
- To prevent a serious threat to health or safety
- To report suspected abuse, neglect, or domestic violence as required by Kentucky law
- For public health activities
- For health oversight activities, such as audits or licensing reviews
- In response to a court order, subpoena, or other lawful process
- To law enforcement as required by law
- To coroners or medical examiners
- For workers' compensation as permitted by law
- For specialized government functions as required by law

**Uses That Require Your Written Authorization**
We will not use or share your information for marketing, sell your information, or share psychotherapy notes without your written authorization. Any other use not described in this Notice requires your written authorization. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent we already relied on it.

**Minors and Personal Representatives**
When a client is under 18, the parent or legal guardian is generally the client's personal representative. The parent or guardian may access the child's records, receive copies, request amendments, and authorize disclosures, except where Kentucky or federal law allows a minor to consent to certain care on their own or otherwise limits parental access. We may ask for proof of legal authority, including a copy of a custody order. If we believe a child may be at risk of harm, we will follow Kentucky law, which may include reporting to the appropriate authorities.

**Your Rights**
You have the right to:
- **Get a copy of your records.** Ask in writing for an electronic or paper copy. We will respond within 30 days and may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee.
- **Ask us to correct your records** if you believe they are incorrect or incomplete. We may say no, but we will explain why in writing within 60 days.
- **Ask for confidential communications**, such as contacting you at a different email or address.
- **Ask us to limit what we use or share.** We are not required to agree, except that we must agree not to share information with a health plan about a service you paid for in full out of pocket, if you ask.
- **Get a list of disclosures** we made in the past six years, with certain exceptions.
- **Get a paper copy of this Notice** at any time.
- **Choose someone to act for you**, such as a health care power of attorney or legal guardian.
- **File a complaint** if you believe your rights were violated (see below).

**Complaints**
You may file a complaint with us using the contact information below, or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights, 200 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, D.C. 20201, 1-877-696-6775, www.hhs.gov/ocr/privacy/hipaa/complaints. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

**Changes to This Notice**
We may change this Notice. Changes apply to all information we hold. The new Notice will be available in the client portal and on our website.

**INFORMED CONSENT FOR CARE: ADULT**

I, the undersigned, voluntarily request and consent to wellness services from Alicia Harrison, MSN, APRN, FNP-C, of Intention Holistic Health PLLC ("the Practice").

**Services.** Services may include a review of my health history, symptoms, diet, and lifestyle; hair tissue mineral analysis (HTMA) and other functional or conventional lab review; and individualized nutrition, lifestyle, and supplement recommendations. Services use a functional and integrative approach focused on root causes, nutrition, and minerals.

**What these services are not.** I understand that these services:
- Are wellness-focused and work alongside, but do not replace, my primary care provider, OB-GYN, midwife, or specialists
- Do not include primary care, urgent or emergency care, after-hours care, or management of chronic disease
- Do not include prescriptions unless the Practice specifically offers and agrees to provide them
- Are not a substitute for recommended screenings, prenatal care, or other conventional medical care

**Testing.** HTMA and functional labs are screening and educational tools. They are not diagnostic tests and do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

**Supplements.** Supplements may cause side effects or interact with medications, pregnancy, or breastfeeding. I agree to tell the Practice about all medications, supplements, allergies, and whether I am pregnant, trying to conceive, or breastfeeding, and to update the Practice if anything changes. I will stop a supplement and contact the Practice (or seek care) if I have a reaction.

**My responsibilities.** I agree to provide complete and accurate information, to maintain a relationship with a primary care provider, and to share results and recommendations with my other providers before making major changes.

**No guarantee.** Individual results vary. No specific outcome is guaranteed.

**Emergencies.** The Practice does not provide emergency care. In an emergency, I will call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. For a mental health crisis, I will call or text 988.

**Kentucky only.** I confirm I am a Kentucky resident and will be physically located in Kentucky during every visit.

**Right to refuse or withdraw.** I may ask questions, decline any recommendation, or withdraw from care at any time.

I have read this consent, had the chance to ask questions, and agree to receive services.

**CONSENT FOR HAIR TISSUE MINERAL ANALYSIS (HTMA)**

**What HTMA is.** HTMA measures minerals and some toxic metals in a small sample of hair. It reflects patterns over roughly the past few months.

**What HTMA is not.** HTMA is a screening and educational tool. It is not a diagnostic test, does not diagnose or treat any disease, and does not replace blood work or other testing my provider may recommend.

**Collection.** I will collect the sample at home following the kit instructions. I understand that hair treatments (dyes, bleach, perms), some shampoos, swimming pools, and not following instructions may affect results. I will tell the Practice about these.

**Laboratory.** My sample and the information needed to process it will be sent to [LAB NAME], a third-party laboratory. The laboratory's results are interpreted by the Practice for wellness purposes.

**Timing.** Kits usually arrive within 5–7 days. Results are usually ready 2–3 weeks after the laboratory receives the sample. Lost, damaged, or insufficient samples may need to be recollected.

**Toxic metals.** If results show elevated toxic metals, the Practice may recommend follow-up testing or care with my primary care provider or my child's pediatrician.

I understand and consent to HTMA testing.
