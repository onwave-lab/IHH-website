## Quick answer

There is no single "best" mineral supplement for every woman, because the right one depends on what you're depleted in, what life stage you're in, and what form your body absorbs well. **For most women preparing for pregnancy or recovering postpartum, the highest-leverage minerals are magnesium glycinate, a whole-food prenatal, zinc picolinate, and electrolytes that include potassium and trace minerals.** The most important decision isn't the brand — it's matching the form to your actual deficit, ideally informed by [HTMA testing](/htma.html) rather than guessing.

## How I evaluate a mineral supplement

Before I recommend anything to a client, I look at:

1. **Form** — magnesium oxide is poorly absorbed; glycinate or malate is better. Iron sulfate causes GI upset; bisglycinate doesn't. Form matters more than dose.
2. **Third-party testing** — supplements aren't FDA-regulated the way pharmaceuticals are, so independent testing for purity and accuracy is non-negotiable. Look for NSF, USP, or certified-GMP.
3. **Excipients and fillers** — magnesium stearate, titanium dioxide, and artificial colors aren't necessary in a well-formulated product.
4. **Real-world tolerability** — the best supplement is the one you'll actually take. If it makes you nauseous or gives you a headache, it's not the right form for you.
5. **Stage-of-life appropriateness** — what works pre-conception may not be what works in early pregnancy or postpartum.

## Best postpartum recovery stack

Postpartum recovery is its own conversation. The minerals most depleted by pregnancy and breastfeeding are magnesium, iron, zinc, and choline. A starter stack worth considering (always in partnership with your provider):

- A whole-food postpartum multivitamin (Needed Postnatal Multi, Designs for Health Twice Daily Multi)
- Magnesium glycinate, evening
- Iron bisglycinate IF blood work confirms deficiency — do not megadose iron blindly
- Electrolytes daily, especially if breastfeeding
- Trace mineral drops in your water

Don't start a postpartum stack from a TikTok influencer's screenshot. Get your mineral status checked first — [HTMA testing](/htma.html) is the most useful single test I run for postpartum women.
