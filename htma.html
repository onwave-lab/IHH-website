The Basics

What HTMA Shows

Your body uses minerals like magnesium, sodium, potassium, and zinc for energy, mood, sleep, and focus. HTMA measures these minerals, and certain heavy metals, in a small sample of hair.

A blood test shows a single moment. Hair grows over time, so HTMA shows patterns from the past few months.

I read your results alongside your health history, food, and sleep to decide where to start.

For Every Season

Before Pregnancy, After Birth & Beyond

Preparing for Pregnancy

Minerals play a role in energy, thyroid, and how your body handles stress. Building them up before you start trying may help you go into pregnancy more nourished.

After Birth

The Mom Years

Busy seasons draw on your stores, too. A plan built on your results helps you focus on what matters most for your energy, mood, and sleep.

HTMA has been studied for decades. Research supports hair as a useful sample for monitoring certain toxic metals, and some studies link hair mineral levels to health markers. It also has limits: results can be affected by hair treatments, collection, and lab methods, which is why it's used as a screening tool. A few examples:

  • EPA Report: The Environmental Protection Agency stated that human hair can be used for biological monitoring of toxic metals, noting that if analyzed correctly, "the data are reliable" (EPA, 1979).
  • Heart Health: A 13-year study found that higher hair mercury levels were linked to a higher risk of acute coronary events (Houston, 2007).
  • Bone Health: Hair magnesium levels were associated with bone mineral density, while serum levels were a weaker indicator (Song et al., 2007).
  • Blood Sugar: Lower hair chromium levels were seen in older adults with type 2 diabetes compared to healthy controls (Stupar et al., 2007).

Research is shared for education only.
