Privacy Policy

Intention Holistic Health PLLC
Last Updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Intention Holistic Health PLLC ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, protects, and discloses your information when you visit www.intentionholistichealth.com ("Website") or engage with our services.

By using the Website or providing information to us, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

1. Company Information

Intention Holistic Health PLLC is a Texas-registered Professional Limited Liability Company (PLLC).

Clinical services are performed by Alicia Harrison, MSN, APRN, FNP-C, licensed in the Commonwealth of Kentucky and state of Texas.

Business operations follow Texas law; clinical care follows Kentucky law and telehealth regulations. Clinical care is not provided in Texas.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information:

  • Non-Personal and Personal Information (Website use)
  • Protected Health Information (PHI) (clinical services only)

2.1 Personal Information (Website and Non-Clinical Services)

When you visit the Website, subscribe to content, join a waitlist, or use non-clinical educational services, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Date of birth (if provided voluntarily)
  • Any information you choose to submit through website forms
  • Payment information processed through secure third-party providers
  • Preferences or interests you share with us

This information is considered general personal information, not PHI, and is not subject to HIPAA.

2.2 Usage Information (Automatically Collected)

We automatically collect information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Pages viewed and actions taken
  • Date and time of visits

This helps us understand Website usage and improve user experience.

2.3 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We may use:

  • Cookies
  • Web beacons
  • Pixels
  • Analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Advertising tools (e.g., Google Ads) for measurement and relevant advertising

These help us understand how visitors use the Website and support relevant advertising. Your data is never sold.

Your choices (opt-out model). Analytics and advertising cookies are on by default. You can opt out at any time using the cookie icon in the bottom corner of any page or the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer, then turning off "Analytics & advertising" and saving. Your choice is stored on your device and applied on future visits. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as an opt-out automatically. You may also disable cookies in your browser settings, though some Website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

2.4 Protected Health Information (PHI) — Clinical Services Only

PHI is collected only when you become a clinical patient of Intention Holistic Health PLLC.

PHI is collected solely through:

  • Practice Better (HIPAA-compliant client portal)
  • HIPAA-compliant telehealth platforms
  • Clinical intake forms and consent documents
  • Forms and documents you upload inside Practice Better

PHI must not be submitted through:

  • Website forms
  • Email
  • Text message
  • Kajabi
  • Social media messaging

Submitting PHI outside secure systems may risk unauthorized exposure.

For a client under age 18, PHI is provided by the child's parent or legal guardian through Practice Better, and the parent or guardian manages the child's account (see Section 7).

Session recordings. With your written consent, results reviews may be recorded. Recordings are shared only through Practice Better, are for your personal use, and are kept only as long as needed to support your care or as required by law. You may decline recording or withdraw consent at any time without affecting your care.

Clinical patients receive a separate HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP).

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 To Provide Website and Non-Clinical Services (Non-PHI)

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Provide non-clinical educational resources and manage waitlists
  • Deliver digital products
  • Send newsletters or marketing emails
  • Improve the Website, services, and user experience
  • Perform administrative and operational functions

3.2 To Provide Clinical Services (PHI)

PHI is used only to:

  • Provide clinical nurse practitioner services
  • Conduct telehealth visits
  • Document care
  • Process labs or referrals (as authorized by you)
  • Comply with applicable healthcare regulations

We do not sell PHI or use it for marketing.

4. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

4.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share non-PHI personal information with vendors who assist with:

  • Website hosting
  • Email delivery
  • Payment processing
  • Marketing automation
  • Administrative services

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their functions.

4.2 HIPAA-Compliant Clinical Services

PHI may be shared with:

  • HIPAA-compliant telehealth platforms
  • Labs (if authorized by you)
  • Healthcare entities involved in your care

PHI is not shared outside secure and compliant systems.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to:

  • Comply with laws and regulations
  • Respond to subpoenas or lawful requests
  • Protect our legal rights

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical, administrative, and physical measures to secure your information.

However, no electronic system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

You acknowledge:

  • Email, Kajabi, and website forms are not HIPAA-secure
  • PHI should only be shared via Practice Better

6. Communication Channels

Text messaging (SMS) is available for general inquiries only. Text messages are not encrypted and are not a secure method of communication.

Do not send protected health information (PHI) via:

  • Text message (SMS)
  • Email
  • Website contact forms
  • Social media messaging

All clinical communication should be conducted through Practice Better, our HIPAA-compliant client portal.

By using our text messaging feature, you acknowledge that SMS is not a secure communication channel and agree not to transmit any personal health information via text.

7. Children's Privacy and Services for Minors

Website. Our Website, newsletters, and social media are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Website forms, newsletters, or social media. Parents and guardians should not submit a child's health information through these channels. If we learn that a child's personal information was submitted through a non-secure channel, we will delete it promptly.

Clinical services for minors. Certain clinical services, including Kids Mineral Insight, are provided to Kentucky residents ages 4–17 only with the written consent of a parent or legal guardian who has authority to consent to the child's care. A parent or guardian takes part in every visit.

How a minor's information is handled.

  • A minor's PHI is collected only through Practice Better, our HIPAA-compliant client portal, and is handled as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.
  • The parent or legal guardian acts as the child's personal representative under HIPAA and receives the child's full results, written report, and plan, at every age, except where Kentucky or federal law requires otherwise.
  • Older children and teens are invited to take part in their own care and to give age-appropriate agreement (assent).
  • If information shared during care raises a concern about a child's safety, we will follow Kentucky law, which may include notifying a parent or guardian or making a report to the appropriate authorities.

8. Your Rights (Clinical Patients)

If you become a clinical patient, you have rights under HIPAA, including:

  • Access to your records
  • Requesting corrections
  • Requesting restrictions on disclosures
  • Requesting confidential communications

These rights are described in your HIPAA NPP.

For a client under age 18, these rights are generally exercised by the parent or legal guardian as the child's personal representative, except where Kentucky or federal law provides otherwise.

9. Your Rights (General Website Users)

You may:

  • Opt out of marketing emails
  • Request access to or deletion of your personal information (non-PHI)
  • Update your contact information

Contact us at the email below for assistance.

  • Texas law for Website use and business operations, and
  • Kentucky law for PHI handling, clinical services, and telehealth requirements for NP services offered in Kentucky

Continued use of the Website constitutes acceptance of any changes.
