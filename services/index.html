Hair Tissue Mineral Analysis (HTMA) is a screening test that measures mineral content in your hair over the past 3-4 months. Unlike blood tests that show a snapshot, HTMA reveals long-term patterns in mineral balance, stress response, and metabolic function—critical factors for hormone health, energy, and fertility.
