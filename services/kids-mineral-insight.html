FOR THE PARENT DOING ALL THE RESEARCH

Maybe it's the sleep. Or the short list of "safe" foods. The tummy troubles, the skin that keeps flaring, or the afternoon meltdowns that seem bigger than the moment. You've read the posts and saved the reels, and you're still not sure where to start.

I'm a mom too, and I'm a family nurse practitioner. When it's my own family, I want real information in hand before I build a plan. That's what Kids Mineral Insight is for: a foundational look at your child's mineral patterns, read alongside how they eat, drink, sleep, play, and handle stress, so the next steps are specific and doable.

Here's what I want you to know up front: a child's symptoms rarely have just one cause. Sleep, development, relationships, school, stress, environment, and medical conditions can all play a role. In this program we focus on the physical foundations (nourishment, minerals, hydration, sleep, and daily routines) while respecting that they're one part of a bigger picture.

WHO KIDS MINERAL INSIGHT IS FOR

A PARENT MIGHT NOTICE

  • Energy that's up and down, or fades early in the day
  • Trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking rested
  • Selective eating, or a very short list of foods they'll accept
  • Sensitivity to stress, transitions, or busy days
  • Muscle tension, cramps, or frequent headaches
  • Constipation or other ongoing tummy complaints
  • Skin that seems to flare, or routines that are hard to keep nourishing

THIS MAY BE A GOOD FIT IF

  • Your child is 4–17 years old and lives in Kentucky
  • They have an established pediatrician or primary-care clinician
  • You'd like a food-first, foundations-first look at what may be contributing
  • You want an individualized plan without signing up for a long program
  • You're ready to try one to three realistic changes at home

None of these mean something is wrong with your child, or that minerals are the explanation. They're simply reasons a parent may want a broader, foundational look.

WHAT HTMA IS, AND WHAT IT ISN'T

HTMA IS

  • A non-invasive screening tool that uses a small hair sample (no needles)
  • A look at mineral patterns that build up over recent months
  • One piece of information, interpreted in context with your child's history, diary, and symptoms
  • A starting point for practical food, hydration, sleep, and routine changes

HTMA IS NOT

  • A diagnostic test
  • Proof of a mineral deficiency or of toxic-metal poisoning
  • A way to diagnose ADHD, autism, anxiety, thyroid or adrenal conditions, or anything else
  • A substitute for your pediatrician's exam or for bloodwork when it's needed

Think of it as a map of areas to explore, not a verdict. Learn more about HTMA.

the process

HOW IT WORKS

Six simple steps, all from home:

Step 1

Screen & Enroll

A brief eligibility screening confirms Kids Mineral Insight is a good fit. Then you'll complete consent and authorization forms in the secure Practice Better portal.

Step 2

Intake & Diary

You'll share your child's history and keep a simple three-day diary of food, drinks, sleep, medications, supplements, and symptoms.

Step 3

Collect & Send

Your kit arrives with step-by-step hair-collection instructions. It's quick, painless, and doesn't involve a blood draw.

Step 4

Interpret

I review your child's results alongside the intake and diary, then prepare a parent-friendly Mineral Foundations Report.

Step 5

Review & Plan

We meet for a 60-minute lab and protocol review. Afterward you'll receive a private recorded walkthrough of the plan to revisit at home.

Step 6

Follow Up

About four to six weeks later, a short follow-up to see what's working, troubleshoot what isn't, and help you keep going.

YOUR 60-MINUTE LAB & PROTOCOL REVIEW

This is where the results turn into a plan. Together we'll:

  • Walk through your child's mineral patterns in plain language
  • Connect them with the diary: meals, drinks, sleep, stress, and routines
  • Talk about your child's strengths, not just the hard parts
  • Choose one meaningful goal with you (and your child, when they're old enough to weigh in)
  • Turn it into one to three realistic actions for the next few weeks
  • Plan around the likely barriers: busy mornings, picky plates, sports schedules, two households
  • If supplements are recommended, map out exactly what to start first, and when to add the next

A PRIVATE WALKTHROUGH YOU CAN REWATCH

After our review, I'll record a private screen walkthrough of your child's written protocol. It's not a generic video lesson. It's me walking through your child's plan, step by step. It's there for the moment you're standing in the grocery store, or when a co-parent or grandparent needs to get on the same page.

WHAT KIDS MINERAL INSIGHT DOESN'T INCLUDE

MEDICAL CARE

  • Well-child exams, vaccinations, or school or sports physicals
  • Prescription-medication management
  • Comprehensive management of chronic pediatric conditions
  • Diagnosis or treatment of psychiatric, behavioral, developmental, infectious, gastrointestinal, or environmental illness based on HTMA

ONGOING SUPPORT

  • Unlimited messaging
  • Repeated protocol revisions
