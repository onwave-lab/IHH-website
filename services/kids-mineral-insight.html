Why Start Here?

The Basics Their Body Runs On

Kids' bodies use minerals for focus, mood, sleep, and growth. When the basics run low, it can show up at the dinner table, at bedtime, and in big feelings.

A small hair sample, collected at home with no needles, shows your child's mineral patterns over the past few months. I read it alongside how they eat, sleep, and play, so your next steps fit your child.

How It Works

01

Test

After a quick fit check and consent forms, your kit ships to your door. Collect a small hair sample at home and mail it in.

02

Review

We meet for an hour to go over your child's results in plain language. You'll get a recording to rewatch anytime.

Plan

You leave with one to three doable steps. Four to six weeks later, we check in to see what's working and fine-tune.
