WHY START WITH A MINERAL TEST?

Minerals help power your energy, mood, sleep, and hormones. When they're out of balance, everything can feel harder.

Hair tissue mineral analysis (HTMA) uses a small hair sample to show your mineral patterns over the past few months. It's done at home, with no needles.

Instead of trial and error, your food, mineral, and supplement plan is built around your results. It's how I built my own plan, too.

A LOOK AT WHAT YOU'LL GET

Your lab report shows the levels of key minerals in your body and how they relate to each other.

On its own, it's a page of bars and numbers. In your review, I translate it into plain language and a step-by-step plan: what to eat more of, which minerals to focus on, and which supplements (if any) to start first.
