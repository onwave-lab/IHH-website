WHAT HTMA SHOWS THAT BLOODWORK ISN'T DESIGNED TO

Standard bloodwork takes a single snapshot. HTMA measures minerals stored in your tissues over the past several months, giving a different view of mineral status, stress patterns, and the foundations your body has been running on.

For the woman whose cycle won't regulate, whose energy disappears by afternoon, or who feels like she hasn't been herself since baby, HTMA can surface patterns standard labs weren't built to find. It isn't the whole picture, but it's a useful place to start.

HTMA is my starting point with most women. When the situation calls for it, I add other functional testing on a patient-by-patient basis.

WHO MINERAL INSIGHT FITS

PREPARING FOR PREGNANCY

  • Cycles that won't regulate, or 40+ days apart
  • Acne that came back in your late 20s
  • Months of trying to conceive without a clear answer
  • You want to enter pregnancy with reserves, not depleted

REBUILDING AFTER BIRTH

  • Postpartum fog that hasn't lifted the way you hoped
  • Energy that disappears by afternoon
  • Hair changes that aren't bouncing back
  • The sense that you haven't felt like yourself since baby

These aren't signs something is wrong with you. They may be signals your body is asking for support it hasn't received yet.
